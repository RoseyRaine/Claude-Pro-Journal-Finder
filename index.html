About & Help

About the Charles Sturt University Journal Finder

The Charles Sturt University Journal Finder helps researchers and staff at Charles Sturt University identify journals that are free or discounted to publish in, and explore journals by quality ranking and field of research.

How to use this tool

Check a journal

Know the journal title or ISSN? Use Check a journal to look it up directly. Results show the journal's rank prominently, its free-to-publish status, and any CSU agreement or discount.

Explore journals

Use Explore journals to discover journals by topic, keyword, or FOR code — then filter by rank, free-to-publish status, or agreement. Note that results are matched by FOR codes and keywords; for deeper discovery, also consult Scimago or a subject database, as scope-based matches may be missed.

Find similar journals

Each journal card includes a Find similar button. This identifies journals sharing the same FOR (Field of Research) codes, ranked by how many codes they have in common, then by free-to-publish status and Scimago quartile. This is a subject-area match, not a citation-based similarity score. Journals without FOR codes will show a "not enough data" message with a link to Scimago.

Share a search

The 🔗 Share button in the filter bar copies a link to your current search and filters to the clipboard. Paste this link to share a pre-filtered view with colleagues — for example, a list of Q1 free-to-publish journals in a specific FOR code area.

Data sources

  • CAUL Open Access Agreements 2026 — Publisher agreements negotiated by the Council of Australian University Libraries (CAUL) that allow Charles Sturt University-affiliated authors to publish open access at no cost or reduced cost. Updated periodically as agreements are renewed or added.
  • Publisher-direct agreements — A small number of journals are included under agreements negotiated directly between Charles Sturt University and a publisher, outside of CAUL. Current publisher-direct agreements include BMJ (25 journals) and the Journal of Medical Microbiology (Microbiology Society). These are maintained manually by Charles Sturt University Library and may not appear in the CAUL title list.
  • Scimago Journal Rankings 2025 — Journal quartile rankings (Q1–Q4) and Diamond Open Access status sourced from the SCImago Journal & Country Rank portal, based on Scopus citation data.
  • CSU Journal Ranking List — An internal Charles Sturt University ranking list maintained by the Division of Library Services, drawing on recognised ranking schemes including ABDC, CORE, and other discipline-specific sources. This list also provides FOR (Field of Research) codes — four-digit codes from the Australian Research Council (ARC) classification system — used to power the FOR code filter and Find Similar feature.
  • CAUL Title List (GitHub) — Journal homepage URLs are sourced from the CAUL Title List repository, matched by ISSN. Not all journals have a recorded URL.

How "Free to Publish" is determined

A journal is marked free to publish in if it appears under a Charles Sturt University CAUL open access agreement (Eligible = Yes) or is a Diamond Open Access journal (no APC for any author). A journal is marked discounted where CSU authors receive a negotiated reduction on article processing charges (APCs). These determinations are based on the data sources listed above and should be verified with the Library before submission.

Creation and maintenance

This tool was created using Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic), with data compilation, review, and quality assurance by Charles Sturt University Division of Library Services. The underlying data is updated when new agreement lists or ranking data are published.
