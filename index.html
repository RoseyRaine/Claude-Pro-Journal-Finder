About this tool

About the CSU Journal Finder

The CSU Journal Finder helps researchers and staff at Charles Sturt University identify journals that are free or discounted to publish in, and explore journals by quality ranking and field of research.

Data sources

  • CAUL Open Access Agreements 2026 — Publisher agreements negotiated by the Council of Australian University Libraries (CAUL) that allow CSU-affiliated authors to publish open access at no cost or reduced cost. Updated periodically as agreements are renewed or added.
  • Scimago Journal Rankings 2025 — Journal quartile rankings (Q1–Q4) and Diamond Open Access status sourced from the SCImago Journal & Country Rank portal, based on Scopus citation data.
  • CSU Journal Ranking List — An internal CSU ranking list maintained by the Division of Library Services, drawing on recognised ranking schemes including ABDC, CORE, and other discipline-specific sources.

How "Free to Publish" is determined

A journal is marked free to publish in if it appears under a CSU CAUL open access agreement (Eligible = Yes) or is a Diamond Open Access journal (no APC for any author). A journal is marked discounted where CSU authors receive a negotiated reduction on article processing charges (APCs). These determinations are based on the data sources listed above and should be verified with the Library before submission.

Creation and maintenance

This tool was created using Claude Sonnet 4.6 (Anthropic), with data compilation, review, and quality assurance by CSU Division of Library Services. The underlying data is updated when new agreement lists or ranking data are published.
